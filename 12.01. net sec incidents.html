Module: Network Security Incidents

From "On Fire" to "Verifiably Clean"

Welcome to the Interactive Incident Management & Response module. A network-based attack abuses the protocols and pathways that make networks work. Your goal is to move from initial panic to a contained, eradicated, and verifiably clean network with a rock-solid paper trail.

🎯

The Goal

To contain, eradicate, and recover network integrity following DoS, MITM, or Unauthorized Access events.

⏱️

The Challenge

Categorizing before containing, identifying the exact OSI layer, and correlating logs within a 5-minute window.

🤝

The Resolution

Coordinating effectively with ISPs and external parties, then implementing NIST-aligned eradication.

Network Threat Matrix

This section breaks down the five primary families of network attacks. Understanding these is crucial because categorizing an attack correctly dictates your playbook. Hover over (or tap) each threat card to reveal its primary signature and the specific team or external party you must involve for containment. Applying the wrong playbook makes things worse.

Credential Abuse

Who You Call

Identity Team + Legal

Triage & Detection Engine

Effective detection relies on correlating logs (Firewall, IDS, Authentication). A single source lies, two correlate, three convict. Response Protocol

When an attack exceeds perimeter capacity, you must escalate. This section covers external coordination and the NIST-aligned Eradication & Recovery phases. Containment stops the bleeding; eradication removes the cause; recovery is a verified return to a known-good state.
